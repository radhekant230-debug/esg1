Service Architecture

Four disciplines. One strategic system.

Marketing is an execution layer that amplifies strategy — not a substitute for it. Every engagement draws from four disciplines, sequenced through a single operating system.

01 — Brand Strategy

The foundation everything else is built on.

Positioning determines whether a business is remembered for the right reasons or overlooked entirely. We build that foundation through evidence, not opinion.

  • Positioning
  • Messaging
  • Identity direction
  • Market & consumer research
  • Competitive analysis

02 — Growth Consulting

Strategy for the business, not just the brand.

Growth is a strategic decision before it is a marketing campaign. We work on the business questions that determine whether growth is sustainable.

  • Business strategy
  • Launch planning
  • Expansion strategy
  • Pricing
  • Go-to-market

03 — Customer Experience

Where positioning becomes something people feel.

A brand is judged by its most inconsistent touchpoint. We design the systems that keep experience consistent as a business grows.

  • Journey mapping
  • SOP design
  • Hospitality experience
  • Loyalty systems
  • Service design

04 — Founder Strategy

The founder is often the brand's first asset.

For many of our clients, the founder's voice carries as much weight as the brand's. We build the strategy behind that authority.

  • Founder branding
  • Authority building
  • Communication strategy
  • Thought leadership

The Consulting Operating System

Every project moves through the same structured journey.

Each framework produces documented outputs, measurable outcomes, and a repeatable methodology — not a one-off deliverable.

  • 01

    ESG Discovery™

    A structured audit of the business, its market, and its customers — the research phase every recommendation is built on.

  • 02

    ESG Brand DNA™

    Positioning, narrative, and identity direction, derived from Discovery rather than instinct.

  • 03

    ESG Experience Blueprint™

    Journey mapping and service design so the brand is consistent at every customer touchpoint.

  • 04

    ESG Growth Engine™

    The business strategy, pricing, and systems that convert clear positioning into sustainable growth.

  • 05

    ESG Launch Blueprint™

    Go-to-market planning for a new offering, location, or venture — sequenced, resourced, and de-risked.

  • 06

    ESG Scale Framework™

    Operational systems and SOPs that let a business grow in size without losing what made it work.

  • 07

    ESG Founder Influence™

    A communication and thought-leadership strategy built around the founder's own authority.

  • 08

    ESG Performance Loop™

    Measurement against the outcomes defined at the start, with a structured cycle for iteration.
