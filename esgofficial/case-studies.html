Case 01 — Positioning & Experience Design

Café Mysa

Hospitality — Café
Challenge

A neighbourhood café with a loyal but small following, competing against louder, better-funded openings nearby without a clear point of view.

Research

ESG Discovery™ mapped the guest base, the surrounding competitive set, and the moments where the café's own service fell short of its food.

Insight

Guests weren't choosing Mysa for its menu alone — they were choosing it as a routine. The brand had never named or designed for that role.

Strategy

A positioning built around "the neighbourhood's second kitchen," with an identity and tone to match, and a service blueprint to make the routine repeatable.

Execution

Identity, in-café signage and menu language, and a revised service SOP delivered through the Experience Blueprint framework.

Business Outcomes

Clearer identity across every touchpoint, a documented service standard the team could run without us, and stronger repeat visitation from the surrounding neighbourhood.

Lessons Learned

In hospitality, the experience is the positioning — a strategy that only lives on paper never reaches the guest.

Case 02 — Brand Strategy & Growth

Dishai

Founder-Led Brand
Challenge

A founder-led brand growing on instinct and word of mouth, with no documented positioning to guide hiring, partnerships, or its next stage of growth.

Research

ESG Discovery™ and Brand DNA™ work surfaced what customers valued in the founder's instincts — and where those instincts hadn't yet been written down.

Insight

The brand's real differentiator wasn't a product feature; it was a point of view the founder held but had never articulated externally.

Strategy

A documented positioning and messaging system built around that point of view, paired with a growth plan sequenced through the Growth Engine™ framework.

Execution

Positioning documentation, messaging guidelines, and a founder communication strategy the team could apply consistently.

Business Outcomes

A repeatable positioning the founder no longer had to reinvent in every conversation, and a growth plan aligned with that identity rather than working against it.

Lessons Learned

Founder-led brands often already have the right instinct — the work is documenting it before growth outpaces it.

Case 03 — Customer Experience & Scale

Biaana

Consumer Brand
Challenge

A brand that had grown quickly, to the point where its customer experience no longer matched the premium positioning it had earned.

Research

Journey mapping under the Experience Blueprint™ framework identified exactly where operations had outpaced the brand's original standards.

Insight

The gap wasn't strategic — it was operational. The brand's promise was right; its systems hadn't scaled to keep it.

Strategy

A Scale Framework™ engagement to rebuild SOPs and service standards so growth stopped eroding the experience it was meant to fund.

Execution

Revised SOPs, a loyalty and service system, and a measurement cadence installed through the Performance Loop™ framework.

Business Outcomes

A customer experience realigned with the brand's positioning, and operational systems built to hold as the business keeps growing.

Lessons Learned

Growth doesn't dilute a brand on its own — it exposes whichever systems were never built to scale.
